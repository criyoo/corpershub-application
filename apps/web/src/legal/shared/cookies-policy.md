## 2. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files placed on a user's device when visiting a website or using certain digital services.

Cookies allow websites and applications to:

* Recognize users
* Remember preferences
* Maintain login sessions
* Improve performance
* Analyze usage patterns
* Enhance security
* Personalize user experiences.

Cookies do not typically contain information that directly identifies an individual, but information collected through cookies may be linked with other Personal Data held by Corpershub.
