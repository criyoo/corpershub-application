## 1. INTRODUCTION

Stratvista Limited ("Corpershub", "Company", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, integrity, availability, and security of Personal Data entrusted to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, use, store, disclose, transfer, protect, and otherwise handle Personal Data belonging to users of the Corpershub Platform.

Corpershub operates a technology-enabled marketplace platform that facilitates visibility, discovery, communication, and matching between National Youth Service Corps ("NYSC") Corps Members and Businesses, Organizations, Government Agencies, Educational Institutions, NGOs, and other entities.

This Privacy Policy applies to:

* Website visitors
* Registered Corps Members
* Registered Businesses
* Employers
* Organization representatives
* Recruiters (where applicable)
* Platform administrators
* Any individual whose Personal Data is processed through the Platform.

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and accepted this Privacy Policy.
