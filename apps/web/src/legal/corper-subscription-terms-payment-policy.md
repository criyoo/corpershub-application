* Verify payment information
* Confirm payment authenticity
* Prevent fraud.

Users warrant that they are authorized to use the payment method submitted.

### Flutterwave Payment Flow

For Flutterwave payments, Corpershub follows this process:

1. You complete payment on the Flutterwave Checkout page.
2. Flutterwave redirects you back to Corpershub.
3. Our backend calls the Flutterwave Verify Transaction API to confirm the payment status.
4. Once Flutterwave returns a successful status, your subscription is activated immediately.
5. You receive access to your subscription features right away.

**Important:** Your subscription is activated only after our backend receives a successful verification response from the Flutterwave API. Do not rely on the redirect page alone to confirm subscription activation.

### Bank Transfer Payments

For Flutterwave bank transfer payments, Flutterwave provides real-time confirmation and reconciliation once the transfer is received. Your subscription activates as soon as Flutterwave confirms the payment.
