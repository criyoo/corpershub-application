# General FAQs

What is corpershub?
corpershub is a technology-driven marketplace platform that connects NYSC corps members with organisations through an intelligent matching system. The platform helps corps members discover suitable companies and helps companies identify qualified corps members whose skills, qualifications, and interests align with their requirements.

Is corpershub affiliated with NYSC?
No.
corpershub is an independent private platform and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, operated by, or managed by the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC).

What problem does corpershub solve?  
corpershub addresses common challenges associated with NYSC placement, including

- Random placement of corps members.
- Difficulty finding suitable PPAs.
- Transportation costs incurred during PPA searches.
- Security risks associated with physical placement hunting.
- Underutilisation of corps members' skills.
- Difficulty organisations face when sourcing suitable corps members.

How does corpershub work?
corpershub uses an intelligent matching system that compares corps member profiles with company requirements and generates compatibility scores. Both parties can view profiles, indicate interest, and communicate directly through the platform.

What is a matching score?
A matching score is a percentage indicating how closely a corps member's profile aligns with a company's requirements. Higher percentages generally indicate stronger compatibility.

Does a higher matching score guarantee placement?
No.
Matching scores only indicate compatibility and do not guarantee placement, interviews, offers, or acceptance.

Does corpershub assign corps members to organisations?
No.
corpershub facilitates discovery and communication between corps members and organisations. Placement decisions remain entirely between the parties involved.

Does corpershub influence NYSC posting decisions?
No.
corpershub has no involvement in NYSC deployment, posting, relocation, or posting approval decisions.

Is my information secure?
Yes.
corpershub implements security measures designed to protect user information and maintain platform integrity.

Can I delete my account?
Yes.
Users may request account deletion subject to applicable legal, operational, and record-keeping requirements.

What payment methods are accepted?
Accepted payment methods are displayed during checkout and may include debit cards, bank transfers, USSD, and other supported payment channels.

What happens if my payment fails?
You may retry the payment or initiate a new payment attempt.
I made payment but my subscription is not active.
Please contact support and provide your transaction reference for assistance.

How do I contact corpershub?
You can contact us through:

- The Contact Us page.
- Email support.
- In-platform support channels.

Is corpershub a recruitment agency?
No.
corpershub is a technology platform that enables direct connections between corps members and organisations.

Does corpershub guarantee placement or employment?
No.
corpershub does not guarantee placement, employment, interviews, acceptance, or job offers.

What should I do if I encounter suspicious activity?
Please report the account or activity immediately through the platform's reporting and support channels.
