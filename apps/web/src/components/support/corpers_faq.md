Corpers FAQs

How do I register as a corps member?
Create an account, verify your email address, complete your profile, and provide the required information.

What information do I need to provide?
You may be required to provide:

- Full name.
- Email address.
- Phone number.
- State of deployment.
- Local Government Area.
- Educational background.
- Course of study.
- Skills.
- Certifications.
- Career interests.
- NYSC details.
- NIN information for verification.

Why do I need to verify my NIN?
NIN verification helps improve trust, authenticity, and security on the platform.

Why do I need to upload my NYSC documents?
Uploaded NYSC documents help verify that you are an eligible and genuine corps member.

Will companies be able to see my profile?
Yes.
Verified organisations can view your profile information to determine whether you are a suitable match.

Can I hide my profile from companies?
Depending on your account settings, you may be able to control your profile visibility.

Can I browse companies before subscribing?
This depends on your subscription plan and platform access level.

How can I improve my matching score?
You can improve your score by:

- Completing your profile.
- Adding relevant skills.
- Uploading certifications.
- Updating educational information.
- Specifying your interests and preferences.

Why is my matching score low?
Low scores may result from:

- Incomplete profile information.
- Different skill requirements.
- Industry mismatch.
- Different location preferences.
- Missing qualifications or certifications.

Can I indicate interest in multiple companies?
Yes.
You may indicate interest in as many companies as permitted by your account type.

Can multiple companies contact me?
Yes.
Multiple organisations may discover and contact you through the platform.

What happens after a company indicates interest in me?
The company may initiate a conversation through the platform to discuss potential placement opportunities.

Can I reject a company that contacts me?
Yes.
You are free to accept or decline any opportunity.

Can I chat directly with companies?
Yes.
corpershub provides real-time messaging functionality between companies and corps members.

Can I update my profile later?
Yes.
You can update your profile information at any time.

What payment methods are accepted?
Accepted payment methods are displayed during checkout and may include debit cards, bank transfers, USSD, and other supported payment channels.

What happens if my payment fails?
You may retry the payment or initiate a new payment attempt.
I made payment but my subscription is not active.
Please contact support and provide your transaction reference for assistance.

What happens if my subscription expires?
Your access to certain features may be limited until your subscription is renewed.

Can I transfer my subscription to another person?
No.
Subscriptions are personal and non-transferable.

Will corpershub find a PPA for me automatically?
No.
corpershub helps you discover opportunities and connect with organisations, but placement decisions remain between you and the organisation.

Can I use corpershub before NYSC camp?
You may create an account if eligible, but certain features may become available only when you are an active corps member.

Can I use corpershub after completing NYSC?
Some features may remain accessible, while NYSC-specific features may no longer apply.

How do I unlock my full corper profile?
Submit and complete biodata, NIN, NYSC call-up, and NYSC state code verification. Once approved, the full profile flow opens.

What if my verification submission fails?
The failed attempt appears in submission history with the actual error. Correct the value and submit again.

How long does support take to respond?
Support chat targets a 1 hour response window. Email support targets a 24 hour response window.

Can support change locked profile fields?
Yes.
If a locked field needs admin intervention, contact support with the exact field and correction required.
