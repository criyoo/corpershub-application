# What Every Prospective NYSC Corps Member Must Know

> A complete guide to preparing for, participating in, and successfully completing the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) programme.

---

# Overview

The National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) is a mandatory one-year national service for eligible Nigerian graduates. The process begins long before orientation camp and continues until you receive your Certificate of National Service (CNS) or Certificate of Exemption.

This guide walks you through everything you need to know before, during, and after your service year.

---

# Phase 1 — Before NYSC Mobilization

## 1. Confirm Your Eligibility

Before preparing for NYSC, ensure that you are eligible.

You should:

- Have successfully completed your academic programme.
- Be cleared by your institution.
- Have your results approved by the Senate/Academic Board.
- Meet NYSC age requirements where applicable.
- Ensure your institution is accredited.

Foreign-trained graduates must also complete NYSC document evaluation.

---

## 2. Obtain Your National Identification Number (NIN)

Your National Identification Number (NIN) is compulsory for NYSC registration.

Apply through:

https://www.nimc.gov.ng/

---

## 3. Ensure Your JAMB Details Are Correct

Locally trained graduates should verify:

- Full name
- Date of birth
- Gender
- JAMB Registration Number

Any discrepancies should be corrected before NYSC registration.

JAMB Portal:

https://www.jamb.gov.ng/

---

## 4. Obtain Your NERD Clearance Slip (If Applicable)

As from 2026, NERD clearance slip is now mandatory for NYSC mobilization, therefor all prospective corp members are required to complete their Nigeria Education Repository and Databank (NERD) registration and obtain a clearance slip before NYSC registration.

Prepare:

- Personal information
- Academic information, including matriculation number
- Degree/Statement of Result
- NIN
- Passport photograph
- Project/Dissertation (where applicable)

Coming soon: Soon corpers will be able to apply for and obtain their clearance slip directly from Corpershub
For now you can visit any of the accredited agents in your state of residence of posting.
You can find a list of agents here[(https://esmat.ned.gov.ng/ai/lookup_nerd_digital_service_centre_eco.php)]

---

## 5. Ensure Your Institution Uploads Your Details

Your institution must upload your details to NYSC before you can register.

Confirm:

- Your name appears on the NYSC Senate List.
- Your details are accurate.

Check here:

https://portal.nysc.org.ng/

---

## 6. Prepare Required Documents

Before registration, have digital and physical copies of:

- National Identification Number (NIN)
- JAMB Registration Number
- Statement of Result or Degree Certificate
- School Identity Card
- Passport photographs
- Birth Certificate or Age Declaration (if required)
- Functional email address
- Active phone number

Foreign-trained graduates should additionally prepare:

- International Passport
- Academic Transcript
- Travel Documents
- Evaluation Documents

---

# Phase 2 — NYSC Online Registration

## 1. Create Your NYSC Account

Register only on the official NYSC Portal.

Official Portal:

https://portal.nysc.org.ng/

---

## 2. Complete Online Registration

Provide accurate information including:

- Personal details
- Academic details
- Contact information
- Next of kin
- Medical information (where required)

Upload:

- Passport photograph
- Required supporting documents

---

## 3. Biometric Capture

Complete biometric verification personally.

Never allow anyone else to complete biometrics on your behalf.

---

## 4. Print Your Green Card

After successful registration, print your:

- Green Card

This document confirms successful registration.

Keep multiple copies.

---

## 5. Wait for Your Call-Up Letter

Once mobilized, download your:

- NYSC Call-Up Letter

It contains:

- State of deployment
- Orientation camp
- Reporting date
- Instructions

---

# Phase 3 — Preparing for Orientation Camp

## Documents to Take

Carry originals and photocopies of:

- Call-Up Letter
- Green Card
- Degree Certificate or Statement of Result
- School ID Card
- Medical Fitness Certificate
- Passport photographs
- Valid Identification

---

Recommended items include:

- Underwear
- Casual clothing

### Personal Items

- Toiletries
- Bucket
- Padlock

- Bedsheets
- Pillow
- Torchlight
- Power bank
- Extension socket
- Water bottle

### Health

- Prescription medications
- Pain relievers
- Hand sanitizer
- Face masks (if required)

### Miscellaneous

- ATM card
- Cash
- Notebook
- Pen
- Phone charger

---

# Phase 4 — Orientation Camp

During the 21-day orientation camp you will:

- Complete camp registration
- Undergo document verification
- Complete biometric verification
- Receive your NYSC kits
- Be assigned a platoon
- Participate in drills
- Attend lectures
- Participate in Skill Acquisition and Entrepreneurship Development (SAED)
- Participate in sports and social activities

---

# Phase 5 — Posting to Your Place of Primary Assignment (PPA)

After camp you will receive:

- Posting Letter
- Place of Primary Assignment (PPA)

Immediately:

- Report to your employer
- Obtain acceptance or rejection
- Submit required documentation

---

# Phase 6 — Relocation (Optional)

You may apply for relocation under approved conditions such as:

- Health
- Marriage
- Security concerns

Applications are submitted through the NYSC Portal.

Supporting documentation is required.

---

# Phase 7 — Community Development Service (CDS)

Throughout the service year you are expected to:

- Join a CDS group
- Attend weekly CDS meetings
- Participate in community projects
- Complete personal CDS projects where applicable

Attendance is mandatory.

---

# Phase 8 — Monthly Clearance

Every month you must:

- Report for biometric clearance
- Complete Local Government clearance
- Obtain employer clearance

Failure to complete monthly clearance may delay your allowance.

---

# Phase 9 — NYSC Allowance

Eligible corps members receive monthly allowance from the Federal Government.

Some employers and state governments may also provide:

- Accommodation
- Feeding
- Transport allowance
- Additional stipends

---

# Phase 10 — SAED Programme

The Skill Acquisition and Entrepreneurship Development (SAED) programme helps corps members develop entrepreneurial skills.

Areas include:

- ICT
- Agriculture
- Fashion
- Construction
- Manufacturing
- Creative Arts
- Business Development

Participation is encouraged.

---

# Phase 11 — Passing Out

At the end of the service year you will:

- Complete final clearance
- Submit required documentation
- Receive your Certificate of National Service (CNS)

Keep this certificate safely.

---

# Important Things Every Corps Member Should Know

## Keep Your Documents Safe

Maintain multiple copies of:

- Green Card
- Call-Up Letter
- Acceptance Letter
- Posting Letter
- Medical Certificate
- Degree Certificate
- School ID
- NIN Slip

Store both digital and physical copies.

---

## Use Only Official Websites

Never register through agents.

Use only official government portals.

---

## Beware of Fraud

Do not:

- Pay unofficial agents
- Share your login credentials
- Submit false documents
- Allow others to complete biometric registration

---

## Stay Updated

Follow announcements from:

- NYSC
- Your Institution
- Student Affairs Office
- NYSC Desk Officer
