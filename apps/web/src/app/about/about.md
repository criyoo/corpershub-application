# About Corpershub

## The Story Behind Corpershub

Corpershub was born from a simple but important question:

Why should talented NYSC corps members struggle to find the right Place of Primary Assignment when organisations are also struggling to find the right people?

Every year, thousands of corps members begin their service year with valuable skills, qualifications, certifications, experience, and career ambitions. Many are developers, designers, accountants, marketers, engineers, healthcare professionals, administrators, educators, analysts, and future business leaders.

Yet, when it comes to placement, many corps members are posted randomly or are forced to search for opportunities by moving from one organisation to another. This process is often stressful, expensive, unsafe, and inefficient.

A skilled software developer may end up in an organisation with no technology needs.
A finance graduate may be placed where their knowledge is barely used.
A marketing talent may spend the service year doing unrelated paperwork.
A qualified corps member may spend weeks searching for a PPA while spending heavily on transportation.

At the same time, organisations that need young, capable, and motivated talent often struggle to find corps members whose skills match their roles, culture, and operational needs.

The problem is not a lack of talent.

The problem is poor connection, poor visibility, and poor matching.

Corpershub was created to solve this problem.

Corpershub is a technology-driven marketplace platform that connects NYSC corps members directly with companies using an intelligent matching system. The platform helps corps members discover organisations that align with their skills and career goals, while helping companies identify corps members who match their talent needs.

No middleman.
No unnecessary stress.
No random search process.
No wasted potential.

Just a smarter way to connect talent with opportunity.

## Who We Are

Corpershub is a digital marketplace designed for NYSC corps members and organisations in Nigeria.

We help corp members connect with companies willing to accept them for their service year, by allowing them to create professional profiles, showcase their skills, view company profiles, see compatibility scores, indicate interest, and connect with the organisations that need their skills and capabilities.

We also help companies connect with corp members with the relevant skill set they desrie by creating business profiles, viewing corps member profiles, seeing matching scores, indicating interest in suitable candidates, and starting real-time conversations with potential corps members.

Our platform acts as the bridge between young talent and organisations looking for capable NYSC corp member that might potentially become valuable hands to their organisation.

## What Makes Corpershub Different?

Corpershub is not a traditional recruitment agency.

We do not stand between corps members and companies, we do not collect CV, organise interview or help corp members find permanent role or jobs. Instead, we provide the technology that allows both parties to find, evaluate, and connect with each other directly.

## Our unique features include

* Verified corp members and registered companies.
* Intelligent matching scores shown in percentages.
* Detailed corps member profiles.
* Detailed company profiles.
* Direct interest indication from both sides.
* Real-time chat between companies and corps members.
* Skill-based discovery and filtering.
* A marketplace model with no physical middleman.
* A safer and more cost-effective alternative to manual PPA searching.
