### Introduction

Corpershub makes it easier for NYSC corps members and companies to find the right match through a simple, transparent, and technology-driven process.

The platform is designed to help corps members discover suitable companies, help companies discover suitable corps members, and allow both parties to connect directly when there is mutual interest.

#### Step 1: Create a Company Account

Companies begin by signing up on Corpershub and creating a business account.

The company provides the necessary registration and contact information.
  
#### Step 2: Complete the Company Profile

Companies complete their profile by providing important details such as:

* Company name
* Industry
* Business type
* Company description
* Office location
* Required skills
* Preferred corps member qualifications
* Available placement roles
* Work structure
* Contact information
* CAC verification details, where required

A complete company profile helps corps members understand the organisation and improves the accuracy of the matching system.
  
#### Step 3: Define Talent Requirements

Companies enter the skills, qualifications, and preferences they are looking for in corps members.

This may include:

* Course of study
* Technical skills
* Soft skills
* Location preference
* Role type
* Industry experience
* Certifications
* Availability
* Career interest

The more specific the company is, the better the platform can recommend suitable corps members.
  
#### Step 4: Discover Matching Corps Members

Corpershub uses its matching system to recommend corps members whose profiles align with the company’s needs.

Companies can view corps member profile cards showing compatibility scores in percentage form.
  
#### Step 5: Review Corps Member Profiles

Companies can click into a corps member’s profile to view more details, such as:

* Education background
* Skills
* Certifications
* NYSC information
* Uploaded documents, where applicable
* Preferred work location
* Career interests
* Experience, where applicable

This helps companies make better and faster decisions.
  
#### Step 6: Indicate Interest

If a company finds a suitable corps member, it can indicate interest.

This helps create a clear signal that the company would like to engage with the corps member.
  
#### Step 7: Start a Real-Time Chat

Companies can initiate a chat with corps members directly on the platform.

This allows the company to discuss the role, expectations, availability, placement process, and any additional requirements.

#### Step 8: Evaluate and Select

After communication, the company can evaluate whether the corps member is a good fit for the organisation.

This may involve internal screening, interviews, document review, or any other company-specific process.
  
#### Step 9: Proceed With Placement

Once both parties are satisfied, the company and corps member can proceed with the appropriate placement arrangement in line with NYSC requirements.

### The Matching Score

The matching score is one of the key features of Corpershub.

It helps both corps members and companies quickly understand compatibility before starting a conversation.

The score may consider factors such as:

* Skills
* Course of study
* Qualifications
* Certifications
* Preferred industry
* Preferred location
* Role requirements
* Company needs
* Corps member interests
* Profile completeness

This makes the process faster, smarter, and more transparent.

### Why Use Corpershub?

* Find suitable corps members faster.
* Access a wider pool of young talent.
* View profile details before engagement.
* Use matching scores to make better decisions.
* Reduce time spent searching manually.
* Connect directly with interested corps members.
* Improve productivity by selecting better-fit candidates.
