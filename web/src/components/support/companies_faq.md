Company FAQs

How do I register my organisation?
Create a company account, complete your company profile, and provide the required verification information.

Why is CAC verification required?
CAC verification helps confirm the legitimacy of organisations and improves trust and safety on the platform.

What information does my company need to provide?

- You may be required to provide:
- Company name
- CAC registration details
- Company description
- Industry
- Office address
- Contact information
- Required skills
- Placement opportunities

Can we browse corps member profiles?
Yes.
Verified organisations can browse eligible corps member profiles.

How are corps members matched to our company?
The matching system compares your requirements against corps member profiles and generates compatibility scores.

Can we search for specific skills?
Yes.
Companies can search and filter corps members based on skills, qualifications, interests, and other criteria.

Are corps members verified?
corpershub may verify email addresses, NIN details, and submitted documents. However, companies should conduct their own due diligence before making decisions.

Can we contact corps members directly?
Yes.
Companies can initiate conversations through the platform.

Can we interview corps members?
Yes.
You may conduct interviews, assessments, screenings, or evaluations based on your internal processes.

Is there a limit to how many corps members we can contact?
No.
There is no limit on the number of corp members you can contact but the numebr you onboard should be dependent on your companies capacity for NYSC corp member placement.

Can we indicate interest in multiple corps members?
Yes.
Companies may express interest in multiple corps members.

Can multiple companies contact the same corps member?
Yes.
Corps members may receive interest from multiple organisations.

Can we remove or update our company profile?
Yes.
Company profiles can be updated at any time.

What happens if our company details change?
You should update your company profile to ensure accurate matching and communication.

Does corpershub conduct background checks on corps members?
No.
corpershub verifies identity and NYSC registration information provided by the corp members against national databases but cannot guarantee the accuracy of all user-provided information.

Can we recruit corps members after their service year?
Yes.
Many organisations use NYSC placements as a talent pipeline for future employment opportunities.

Can we post multiple opportunities?
Yes.
Companies may create multiple opportunities subject to platform policies and account limitations.

What happens after we identify a suitable corps member?
You may initiate communication, conduct your evaluation process, and proceed with any placement arrangements directly with the corps member.

Are placement agreements handled by corpershub?
No.
Any placement arrangements, interviews, agreements, or engagements are entered into directly between the company and the corps member.

How do company profiles become visible to corpers?
Once company registration information is verified and company profile is completed, your listing will appear in privacy-safe search results and attract relevant corpers.

Why can't I see full corper identity immediately?
Corper discovery is privacy-safe by design. Detailed identity stays limited until the workflow progresses.

How long does support take to respond?
Support chat targets a 1 hour response window. Email support targets a 24 hour response window. We are working to improve these response times.

Can support help with company profile edits?
Yes.
If a locked company field needs correction, send the exact issue through support so the team can review it.
